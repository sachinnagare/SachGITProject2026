Sinhagad Fort is a famous historical fort near Pune.
It was earlier known as Kondhana Fort.
The fort is closely associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the brave warrior Tanaji Malusare.
Sinhagad is located on a hill and offers beautiful views of the surrounding area.
Today, it is a popular place for trekking, history, and sightseeing.
